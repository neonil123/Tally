Privacy Policy — Tally

Effective date: 2026-10-14  ·  Last updated: 2026-10-14

Tally (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal-finance app published by NeoSystems. This policy explains what data the app handles, when it leaves your device, and your choices. Questions or requests: contact@neosystems.cc.

Our core principle: local-first

Tally works fully offline. All of your finance data is stored on your device by default. Cloud features (account sign-in, sync, AI receipt cleanup, family sharing, push notifications) are optional. AI receipt cleanup is part of the receipt-scanning service and is on by default for signed-in users — you are shown exactly what is sent before your first scan, and you can turn it off on that screen or anytime in Settings → Cloud AI. Redacted diagnostics & usage analytics (no amounts, merchants, notes or emails) are also on by default — shown on the same screen and switchable off there or in Settings. Receipt sharing for training is off by default and opt-in. If you never sign in, no personal or financial data leaves your device.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not access other apps installed on your device.

Third-party processors

When cloud features are used, data is processed by:

  • Amazon Web Services — Cognito authentication, AppSync/DynamoDB sync, S3 image storage, and AWS Bedrock (Anthropic Claude) for AI, in the EU region.
  • Resend — sends your account verification and password-reset codes by email (receives your email address and the one-time code), EU region.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and entitlements (receives your account identifier and purchase/subscription state).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications (receives a device push token).

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Data is encrypted in transit.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can, at any time, from Settings:

  • Export my data — download your transactions and categories as JSON.
  • Delete all my cloud data — erase your data from the cloud (your device keeps its copy).
  • Delete account — permanently erase your account and cloud data, including your sign-in account and any uploaded receipt images. Items you shared with your family are retained for those family members.

You can also request deletion without the app: see Data Deletion.

Children

Tally is not directed to children. It is not intended for users under 16 in the EU/EEA (or the digital-consent age set by your country, where lower — minimum 13), or under 13 elsewhere.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR), you have rights to access, export, correct, and delete your data, and to withdraw consent. The in-app tools above cover access/export/deletion; for anything else, contact contact@neosystems.cc.
